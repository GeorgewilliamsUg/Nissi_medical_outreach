Medical Mission

Restoring Hope in Northern Uganda: The Gulu Mission

In partnership with Next Generation Ministries, our team embarked on a four-day intensive medical mission to the remote villages of Gulu District. Our team provided services including general consultations, laboratory testing for malaria, and basic surgical procedures. Beyond medicine, we engaged in spiritual outreach, treating the whole person—body and soul.

Key Outcome: Over 1,200 residents received direct medical intervention, and local leaders were trained in basic first aid protocols to ensure long-term community resilience.

Empowerment & Education

The Namutumba Project: Dignity Through Hygiene

We addressed the silent barrier of "Period Poverty" in Namutumba. By teaching young women how to manufacture their own reusable hygienic solutions from locally sourced flannel, we ensure the sustainability of the project and keep girls in school.

Impact: 300 girls at Cetkana Primary School received menstrual hygiene kits and training, significantly reducing absenteeism and fostering a culture of health and openness.

Community Health Camp

Gingo Village: Addressing Chronic Disease

In a single day in Mityana District, we served 459 citizens. Through our partnership with Live in Light Ministries, we distributed hundreds of pairs of prescription eyeglasses and provided screening for hypertension and diabetes.

The Result: 120 pairs of eyeglasses distributed; 100% of patients received free medication and lifestyle counseling for non-communicable diseases.
