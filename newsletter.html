Stories from the Field

Menstrual Health

Empowering Girls in Namutumba District

Under the guidance of Sylivia and Betty, we conducted an interactive reusable sanitary pad making activity. Participants worked together to create their own pads while sharing experiences and challenges related to menstrual health and hygiene.

Namutumba 2024
Inclusive Care

Breaking Barriers: Outreach to the Deaf Society

We successfully bridged the gap for the deaf community, who often face hurdles like lack of interpreters and privacy issues in hospitals. Our outreach provided accessible, private, and consistent medical care to those who need it most.

Specialized Care 2024
Medical Mission

Four Days of Healing in Gulu District

In partnership with Next Generation Ministries and Dr. Rebecca Hunter, we spent four days serving remote villages. Together with volunteers from Sozo Dream Foundation and Hummingbird Foundation, we provided patient-centered care and shared the love of Christ.

Gulu District Last Week
Education & Hygiene

Supporting Cetkana Primary School

Adolescent girls benefited from our Reusable Sanitary Towel Project. Thanks to donated flannel and machines, we offered comprehensive menstrual hygiene education and supplies. The joy on the ladies' faces was a testament to the impact of this support.

Gulu 2024
Community Health

459 Lives Touched in Gingo Village

Our free medical camp in Mityana district served 459 citizens with BP checks, HIV testing, dental care, and free eye glasses donated by Live in Light Ministries. It was heartening to see the community line up in big numbers for health awareness and aid.
